**Raf's and the Date-Night Table Where the Sides Steal Attention**

Tucked into a bustling New York neighborhood, Raf’s has become a go‑to spot for couples looking to share more than just a meal—it’s where the humble side dishes often become the stars of the evening. If you’ve ever wondered how a plate of roasted carrots or a bowl of whipped feta can steal the spotlight from the entrée, this is the place to see it in action.

## Why It Matters

In a city brimming with flashy mains and over‑the‑top desserts, Raf’s reminds us that thoughtful, vegetable‑forward sides can elevate a date night from pleasant to memorable. The restaurant’s focus on sharing plates encourages conversation, experimentation, and that delightful moment when you realize the “side” is actually the highlight.

## The Vibe: A Date‑Night Destination

Raf’s official site describes the space as warm, intimate, and perfect for lingering over drinks and small plates—a setting designed for couples who want to unwind without the pressure of a formal tasting menu【https://www.rafsnewyork.com/】. Low lighting, communal tables, and a modest bar create a relaxed backdrop where the food does the talking.

> **💡 Pro Tip:** Arrive a little early to snag a corner table; the quieter nooks let you hear each other over the clink of glasses while you sample the menu.

## The Menu: Sides That Shine

While Raf’s offers a range of grilled meats and fresh fish, its menu (viewable online) emphasizes mezze‑style small plates that are built for sharing【https://www.rafsnewyork.com/menu】. Think creamy hummus topped with za’atar, smoky baba ganoush, and seasonal vegetable preparations that change with the market. The kitchen treats sides with the same care as its mains, often highlighting textures—crisp, charred, silky—to keep each bite interesting.

> **⚠️ Watch Out:** Because the vegetable‑focused dishes rotate frequently, it’s a good idea to check the current menu online before you go or ask your server what’s freshest that night.

## What to Order

If you’re aiming for that “side‑steals‑the‑show” experience, consider starting with a trio of dips (hummus, labneh with herbs, and roasted red pepper spread) served with warm pita. Follow up with a vegetable‑centric plate such as charred broccolini with lemon‑almond gremolata or roasted cauliflower tossed in tahini and parsley. For protein, the grilled chicken kebabs or the spiced lamb kofta are solid choices that let the sides remain the conversation starters. As always, verify the exact offerings on the day of your visit, as the menu evolves.
