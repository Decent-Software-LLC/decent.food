**Langer's Deli and the Counter Serving a Better Lunch**

Tucked into the bustling streets of Los Angeles, Langer’s Deli has been dishing out towering pastrami sandwiches that feel like a warm hug from the city itself. If you’ve ever wondered where to find a lunch that’s both humble and legendary, this counter‑service spot is a delicious place to start.

## Why It Matters  
Langer’s isn’t just another deli—it's a touchstone for LA’s sandwich culture, where the pastrami is hand‑cut, slow‑steamed, and piled high on fresh‑baked rye. The counter‑service setup lets you watch the magic happen, turning a simple lunch break into a mini‑celebration of craft and community.

## The Legacy of the Pastrami  
The pastrami at Langer’s is cured in-house, smoked over hardwood, and steamed until it’s melt‑in‑your‑mouth tender. This method, refined over decades, gives the meat its distinctive peppery crust and juicy interior—a profile that’s earned the deli a devoted following among locals and visitors alike.  

> **🎯 Key Insight:** The secret isn’t just the spice rub; it’s the steam‑then‑slice technique that keeps every slice moist and flavorful.

## The Counter Experience  
Ordering at the glass‑front counter feels like stepping into a nostalgic kitchen: you call out your number, watch the sandwich get assembled, and hear the friendly banter of the staff. The space is modest—formica tables, vintage signage, and the constant aroma of rye bread toasting—but that simplicity puts the focus squarely on the food.

## What to Order  
According to the menu, the house‑special **#19** (hot pastrami sandwich with mustard on rye) is the go‑to choice for first‑timers, while the **#12** (pastrami and Swiss with Russian dressing) offers a melty twist for those who crave a little extra richness. Both are served with a pickle spear and a side of crispy fries or a cup of the deli’s beloved matzo ball soup—options that are all listed on their public menu.  

> **💡 Pro Tip:** Ask for the pastrami “extra lean” if you prefer less fat, or “extra fatty” for the indulgent, melt‑in‑your‑mouth experience—staff are happy to customize.

## Practical Details (Hot Spot Showcase)  
- **Location:** Langer’s Deli, Westlake neighborhood, Los Angeles, CA.  
- **What to order:** The #19 hot pastrami sandwich (or #12 pastrami‑Swiss) – both documented on the official menu.  
- **When to go:** Weekday lunch hours (roughly 11 am–2 pm) tend to have shorter lines; weekends can draw a crowd, so arrive early if you prefer a quicker bite.  
- **Who it’s for:** Anyone craving an authentic, no‑frills pastrami experience—from office workers on a quick break to tourists seeking a true LA taste.  
- **What makes it distinct:** The combination of house‑cured, steam‑kissed pastrami, freshly baked rye, and a bustling counter‑service vibe that lets you see every step of the sandwich’s creation.

## Key Takeaways  
- Langer’s Deli offers a quintessential LA pastrami sandwich crafted with in‑house curing and steaming.  
- The counter‑service layout provides a transparent, engaging ordering experience.
