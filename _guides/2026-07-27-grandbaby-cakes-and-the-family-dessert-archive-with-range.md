## Why It Matters
In a world where food trends flash and fade, Grandbaby Cakes offers something steadier: a living record of family dessert recipes that span generations and cultures. Jocelyn’s work reminds us that behind every great bake is a story, and preserving those stories keeps our culinary heritage alive—one layer, frosting swirl, and sprinkle at a time.

## The Creator Behind the Cakes
Jocelyn Delk Adams is the founder, photographer, recipe developer, and voice behind Grandbaby Cakes. ## The Family Dessert Archive
What sets Grandbaby Cakes apart is its “Family Dessert Archive”—a collection of recipes that Jocelyn has gathered from her mother, grandmother, and extended family, then tested, photographed, and written up for home bakers. The archive isn’t just a list; it’s presented with personal anecdotes, holiday memories, and tips that reflect the cultural blend of her Jamaican‑American upbringing. For example, her post on “Grandma’s Sweet Potato Pie” includes a note about how the dish always appears at Thanksgiving gatherings, accompanied by a photo of the handwritten recipe card she inherited. This approach turns each recipe into a miniature heirloom.

## Where Her Voice Fits in Food Culture
Jocelyn sits at the intersection of heritage cooking and modern digital storytelling. While many dessert creators focus on viral aesthetics, she emphasizes authenticity and intergenerational connection. Her work has been featured in outlets like *Essence* and *The New York Times* for highlighting Black culinary traditions in the dessert space—a niche that often goes under‑represented. By marrying polished food photography with heartfelt storytelling, she offers a model for how creators can honor their roots while reaching a broad, diverse audience.
