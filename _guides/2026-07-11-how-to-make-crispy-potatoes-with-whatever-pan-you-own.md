## The Secret to Crispy Potatoes (It’s Not Just Oil)  

Before we dive into pans and techniques, let’s talk starch. Potatoes are basically starch bombs, and how you handle that starch determines your outcome. Soaking sliced potatoes in cold water removes excess starch, which prevents them from sticking and helps them crisp up. But here’s where most people go wrong: **they skip the drying step**. Pat those spuds *bone dry* before they hit the pan. Moisture is the enemy of crispiness!  

> **💡 Pro Tip:** Use a salad spinner or clean kitchen towel to dry your potato slices. It’s a game-changer.

## Pan Selection: Don’t Worry, You’ve Got This  

You don’t need a $200 skillet to make crispy potatoes. Here’s how to work with what you’ve got:  

- **Cast Iron:** The gold standard for even heat retention. Preheat it well, and you’ll get a restaurant-level crust.  
- **Non-Stick:** Great for beginners! Use a lower heat to avoid damaging the coating.  
- **Stainless Steel:** A happy middle ground. It conducts heat well but can stick if you’re not vigilant.  

> **⚠️ Watch Out:** Avoid using too much oil. A thin layer is all you need—this isn’t deep frying!

## The Crispy Potato Technique, Step by Step  

**Ingredients:**  
- 2 large potatoes (Yukon Golds or Russets work best)  
- 2–3 tbsp neutral oil (avocado, grapeseen, or vegetable)  
- Salt (plus any seasonings you love—paprika, garlic powder, rosemary, etc.)  
- Optional: Garlic, herbs, or cheese for finishing  

**Steps:**  
1. **Prep Your Potatoes:** Slice into ½-inch thick rounds or wedges. Soak in cold water for 10 minutes.  
2. **Dry Thoroughly:** Spin or pat dry. *No shortcuts here!*  
3. **Heat the Pan:** Place your pan over medium-high heat. Add oil and let it shimmer (that’s your cue it’s ready).  
4. **Cook in Batches:** Don’t overcrowd! Work in batches, leaving space between each piece.  
5. **Flip with Confidence:** After 4–5 minutes, when edges look golden, flip once. Cook 3–4 minutes more.  
6. **Season Immediately:** Sprinkle with salt and any extras while still hot.  

> **🎯 Key Insight:** Patience is key. Let the potatoes develop color without moving them too soon.

## Serving Ideas & Substitutions  

- **Serve With:** Fried eggs, avocado toast, or as a side to grilled meats.  
- **Substitutions:** Swap oil for clarified butter for extra flavor (watch heat to avoid burning).  
- **Vegan?** Use a high-smoke-point oil like refined coconut oil.
